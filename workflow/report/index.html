    02 / DIVERSITY AND DOMINANCE

    Diversity and dominance

    Diversity describes how read support is distributed among receptor groups. IGH uses clones inferred within each donor and scope. Other chains use single-chain clonotypes.

    Rarefaction compares recovered support at a common depth. It does not estimate missed cells or repeat assembly. If five or fewer groups are recovered, the top-5 share is 100%.

    Depth and rarefaction diagnostics

    For inverse Simpson, the target-depth adjustment is algebraic. Agreement with the observed value is not independent validation.

    Which V and J genes are used?

    Compare each library's share of assigned receptor groups or read support in one gene equivalence group. Receptor groups are IGH clones or single-chain clonotypes at other loci. A gene equivalence group joins genes linked by identical complete reference alleles, directly or through another gene. It sums their single-gene and composite rows for display; it does not merge their receptor groups.

    Additional diversity and saturation measurements

    The estimated asymptote A comes from the recovered support vector. At positive support depth m, target-depth inverse Simpson is m / (1 + (m - 1) / A), the reciprocal of expected Simpson concentration. For A greater than 1, the continuous curve reaches 95% of A at 19 (A - 1). Round up to a positive integer for a support depth. With A = 1, every positive depth already reaches the plateau. This is not a required FASTQ depth, and plotted curves stop at observed support. An asymptote is unavailable below two support units or when every group has one unit.

    03 / ISOTYPES AND MUTATION

    Isotype composition and somatic hypermutation

    Compare isotype proportions and somatic hypermutation (SHM), the scored changes relative to an assigned germline sequence.

    Proportions use support with a callable class or subclass. IgG:IgA requires positive support for both classes; it does not count class-switch events.

    Each eligible clone has equal weight. A zero median can include mutated members and does not establish a naive cell state. Whole V covers FWR1 through FWR3, including CDR1 and CDR2, and excludes CDR3.

    How does coverage affect SHM availability?
    Mutation-based selection estimates

    BASELINe compares mutations that change amino acids with a model of mutation targeting. Positive sigma means a higher estimated frequency of these changes than the model expects; negative sigma means a lower frequency. Bars show 95% posterior intervals under that model. These exploratory estimates do not measure binding affinity or establish response maturity.

    04 / SHARING BETWEEN LIBRARIES

    Sharing between libraries

    Each point compares two libraries from the same donor. IGH comparisons use inferred clones; other chains use single-chain clonotypes. Select a pair to inspect its groups or highlight its libraries across chapters.

    Bray-Curtis measures redistribution of recovered support across receptor groups. A positive Simpson difference means library B is more concentrated than library A. Directional group plots require distinct supplied visit order. Neither measure establishes absolute cell expansion or contraction.

    Compare donor measurements with a reference timepoint

    Each point compares a reference library with a later library from the same donor. Panels and categorical colours use reference-library metadata; hover shows both libraries. Separate reference libraries remain separate. Lines join successive visits only when each has one available comparison. Missing or repeated follow-up observations interrupt the line. The horizontal axis shows supplied visit order, not elapsed time.

    Pair and donor inclusion for the selected measurement

    Counts describe the current measurement and filters. A donor can have both included and excluded pairs.

    Morisita-Horn compares relative read support; Jaccard is the fraction of distinct groups detected in both libraries out of those detected in either. Both range from 0 (no overlap) to 1 (complete overlap by that measure). Nondetection can reflect shallow recovery. Repeated detection alone does not establish cell persistence or migration.

    Observed differences among shared groups

    Each shared group has one vote. Tiny differences count equally with large ones, and shallow recovery can reverse their sign. Equal means the same observed proportion in both libraries.
